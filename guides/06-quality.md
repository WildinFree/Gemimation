# Quality review

Judge actual artifacts, not the richness of the written plan. A polished still cannot prove animation, synchronization, a loop or clean transitions. Use the best playback, frame extraction and measurement capabilities available, stating any material gaps.

## Review in stages

| Stage | Inspect | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| Timing/storyboard | recording, beats, opening, hook/turn/payoff and loop | meaning and action fit the real audio; no invented narration |
| Cast | front, three-quarter, profile/back where seen, black silhouettes, resting face, key reactions, gait/gesture | distinct identity and appeal; all views and attached parts work |
| Look/props | representative scene and hero props at nearest planned scale, in the required mood | materials, linework, proportions, lighting and period agree |
| Moving takes | first/middle/last, all camera arrivals, state changes and fast-action/contact ranges | readable complete acting, camera purpose, grounded relationships |
| Sound | audible cue-to-picture checks and every speech section | voice preserved/clear; events synchronized; no intrusive bed |
| Final | complete encoded video, all accessible frames, boundaries and replay | no unresolved faults; all required content and quality survive export |

For black silhouettes, shrink the cast to phone-thumbnail size and judge who, facing and action without colour/face detail. Main characters must remain distinguishable. Also compare with hats removed where headwear is carrying too much identity.

Review every visible frame when frame access is available, using numbered contact sheets for navigation and full-resolution checks for detail. Always play the complete video. Inspect the first/last three frames of each take, each transition and two frames either side, the first/last replay frames, all flagged frames and six-frame runs around fast collisions. Contact sheets with sampled frames cannot certify unseen intervals.

Where frame access is unavailable, use repeated playback, slows/scrubs and visible reference frames. State that exact frame coverage was unavailable; do not claim an all-frame pass.

## Reject and repair these faults

- **Meaning:** the viewer cannot identify the action, cause, mechanism or consequence; the gag needs a label.
- **Design drift:** changing face/hair/clothes, equal indistinct cast silhouettes, inconsistent height/prop scale, unintended style changes.
- **Drawing:** missing hands, fingers, disconnected limbs, facial features off the head, brows in hair, changing limb lengths, broken profile views.
- **Contact:** floating/sunk feet, sliding stance foot, loose grip, floating hat, chair/rider mismatch, broken hinges or disappearing pieces.
- **Depth:** a person inside furniture, effect cut by a wall, flickering overlaps, smoke through ceilings, foreground fragment that reads as a random blob.
- **Motion:** one-frame teleports, accidental freeze, linear sliding instead of an acted action, unsignalled facing flip, repetitive idle wobble.
- **Camera:** aimless roaming, missed arrival, unblurred teleport, lens inside geometry, impact wobble lasting too long, needlessly searching reframes.
- **Transitions:** unmatched double images, reversed direction, loss of eye trace, arbitrary cover, pointless cut, visible replay seam.
- **Look:** photo textures dominating, flat boxes, faceted close-ups, black/crushed shade, glow without a source, glowing faces, noisily reflective metal.
- **Effects:** tiny unreadable accents, identical particles, stock glitter/fire, long lingering effects, obscured speaking face or story object.
- **Sound:** wrong-word mouth movement, repetitive flap, contact/SFX mismatch, masked voice, altered narration, click or unwanted ending sting.
- **Export:** wrong orientation/duration, cropped essential action, blank/frozen frames, absent audio, low-quality upscale presented as native detail, captions/labels.

A tangent is a readability fault: a pole seeming to grow from a head, a hand merging with a torso or two contours exactly touching. Reposition for clear separation or intentional overlap. Check innocent objects that could read as something else at thumbnail size; show the relevant mechanism/interior in a close-up.

## Calibrate appearance

Use these as diagnostics when measurement tools are available, not as mandatory analysis overhead or universal skin targets.

- Day images have coloured midtones, visible ink darks and near-white highlights: median display luma often around 0.40-0.57; night around 0.20-0.38.
- Day shade retains hue, approximately 0.45-0.55 of lit display luma on the same surface. Golden shadows are violet-blue, never muddy brown.
- Skies remain saturated, sunset gradients avoid a grey band, and far scenery is lighter/less contrasty than the action plane.
- Lines remain smooth at full resolution. Material highlights distinguish metal/glaze from matte cloth/wood.
- Bright faces, deep skin and dark costumes all remain readable without erasing their actual colours.
- Motion and detail do not compete with the one subject the camera is asking the viewer to notice.

## Efficient repair without lowering quality

Keep faults as short entries: timestamp/frame, visible problem, affected character/object, proposed fix and recheck range. Repair the cause, then inspect its neighbours and any shared element changed by the fix. A lighting/character change may affect the whole film.

Use preview resolution and selected frames for early iteration. Inspect final output at its actual resolution; a preview can hide line, grip and clipping faults. Reuse unchanged checks only when they still describe the current artifact. After changes, replay the complete final film and inspect the full available frame set again.

If a requested technique cannot be executed reliably, choose another technique serving the same narrative purpose. Preserve identity, timing, clarity and emotional force instead of accepting a broken ambitious effect.

At delivery provide the actual artifact, its duration/resolution when known, required sound credits, and a concise note on any unverified quality or capability limit. Do not report "all passed" when checks were inferred.
